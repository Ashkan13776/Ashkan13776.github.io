About Research Publications CV

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Vanderbilt University, working in the Machine Intelligence and Neural Technologies (MINT) Lab advised by Prof. Soheil Kolouri.

My research focuses on attention mechanisms, large language models, and geometric deep learning, often guided by ideas from applied mathematics and optimal transport. I am also interested in neural simulators and digital twin applications.

Research

I work on attention mechanisms and representation learning, with a focus on transport based structured attention, linear and kernelized transformers, and geometric neural simulators for digital twin applications.

Recently, I was an ML PhD intern on the Content AI team at Instacart, working on cost efficient LLM cascades and confidence estimation for large scale catalog attribute extraction.
